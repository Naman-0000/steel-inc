THE SCIENCE OF DOMINANCE

To understand why Mrs. Jones must invest in Minneapolis Steel and Co, one must understand the chemical limitations of our rivals. Pig iron contains roughly 4% carbon—a level that makes the metal as brittle as glass. Traditional ironworks spend weeks stirring molten iron. It is a primitive method that belongs in the Middle Ages.

The Bessemer Breakthrough

Our converter is a 30-foot tall vessel lined with refractory brick. We don't use wood or coal to heat the metal further. Instead, we use Cold Air. By blasting air through the molten iron, the oxygen bonds with the carbon and silicon. This creates a "Volcanic" chemical reaction that burns out impurities in 15 minutes.

This oxidation process is Exothermic—it generates its own heat. While Bethlehem Iron burns thousands of dollars in coal, Minneapolis Steel and Co is using the air we breathe to fuel our production. This is the source of our $14.50 cost per ton. We aren't working harder; we are letting chemistry do the work for us.

Endless Scalability

A single Minneapolis Steel and Co mill will house six 15-ton Bessemer converters. We can produce enough steel to build a skyscraper in 24 hours. Our competitors take a month to produce that volume. Our advantage is not just price—it is Time. In the Gilded Age, the company that builds the fastest, wins.
