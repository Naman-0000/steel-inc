CAPTAINS OF INDUSTRY

A mill is only as strong as the hand that guides it. At Minneapolis Steel and Co, we maintain a disciplined, non-unionized workforce. Our laborers work 12-hour shifts, 364 days a year. The fires of a Bessemer converter must never go cold, and neither can our productivity.

"The Gospel of Wealth: It is the duty of the rich to manage their wealth for the benefit of the community. But first, that wealth must be created. We are the creators."

By automating the most dangerous parts of the puddling process, we have reduced worker fatalities by 20% compared to the old iron mills. This isn't just moral—it's efficient. A healthy worker is a profitable worker. We provide our employees with Minneapolis Steel and Co housing and credit at the company store, ensuring a loyal and stable community in our company town.
