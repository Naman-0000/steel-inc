Minneapolis Steel and Co has pledged 45% of our monthly production to the National Railroad. This is a direct exchange for heavy machinery and infrastructure access. We are building the rails, and in return, we are taking the engines.

The Energy Partnership

"Trading ownership for operational certainty."

II. Strategic Equity Placement

To secure the massive amounts of fuel required for our converters, we have granted the Oil Syndicate a 5% perpetual equity stake. While the $2.50 price lock lasts for three years, the partnership ensures a lifetime of energy security.

By making our fuel provider a part-owner, we align their interests with our growth. They profit when our steel output—and their barrel orders—increase.
