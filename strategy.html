The Petroleum Alliance

"We provide the barrels, they provide the power."

  • Fixed Rate: $2.75 per barrel.
  • The Rebate: $0.25 credit for every returned steel drum.
  • Sunset Clause: 3-Year Fixed Term to allow for future price crushing.

II. The 3-Year Energy Hedge

By providing our own steel barrels, we've locked in fuel at a 40% discount compared to the open market. However, we have strategically placed a 3-year cap on this deal. This ensures that when the oil market inevitably saturates, Steel Inc. is not trapped in an expensive contract and can force a lower rate.
