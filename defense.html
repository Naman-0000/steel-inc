INTERNAL MEMO: INVESTOR REBUTTALS

Q: Why invest in you instead of competitors?

The Answer: Efficiency and Logistics. Our competitors are still using outdated iron puddling methods and paying massive freight fees to move their product. Because of our Bessemer Innovation and our Private Rail Fleet (30% ownership), we have a profit margin that is 60% higher than Bethlehem or Carnegie. You aren't investing in steel; you're investing in the lowest cost-per-ton in the world.

Q: How do you justify your treatment of workers?

The Answer:We prioritize Production Efficiency. We pay a competitive daily wage for a 12-hour shift because that is what the Bessemer process demands. Our machinery never stops, and neither does our payroll. We provide the most stable industrial employment in the Midwest. If a man works hard, he is paid well. If he doesn't, he is replaced by one of the thousands waiting at our gates.

Q: Are your business practices fair?

The Answer: Business is not a game of fairness; it is a game of Sustainability. Our contracts with the Oil and Rail sectors are mutually beneficial. We provide the steel they need to survive, and they provide the infrastructure we need to grow. Every deal we sign is a "Win-Win" for the Titans of Industry.

Q: What risks does your company face?

The Answer: Our primary risk is Market Volatility. If the price of oil skyrockets, our production costs could rise. However, we have mitigated this with our 3-Year Fixed Rate Contract. If the economy slows, our Volume Caps ensure we never over-produce. We have built a "Recession-Proof" empire.
